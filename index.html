About us

The Legacy of Engineer Ragai El Shahawi

Engineer Ragai El Shahawi built a legacy rooted in engineering, entrepreneurship and a belief in creating lasting value. Beginning his career in the 1970s, he established a foundation in construction and residential development, contributing to the growth of Cairo’s urban landscape through projects in Maadi and other neighborhoods.

His vision extended beyond individual buildings. Through partnerships and a willingness to explore new opportunities, he helped expand the family’s work into tourism, coastal development and building materials. His involvement in projects such as Ein Resort and Ein Bay reflected an ambition to create destinations where architecture, quality and lifestyle came together.

His business interests also reached into cement, contracting and ready-mix concrete, connecting the materials of construction with the development of communities. Together, these ventures helped establish a broad foundation for the family’s business and the generations that followed.

Today, the Shahawi name continues to be associated with development and entrepreneurship. His legacy lives on not only in the projects and businesses connected to his career, but also in the vision and spirit of enterprise he passed on to his family.

Milestones of the Shahawi Group

  • 1976: The group is established
  • 1984: El Shahawi Engineering & Contracting, a general contracting company
  • 1997: Galalah, a touristic development company
  • 2001: El Ain Swan, a touristic development company
  • 2008: Premix, a ready-mixed concrete company
  • 2008: Palmaria, a touristic development company
  • 2016: Ein Hills, a touristic development company

Previous projects

Ein Resort

Ein Sokhna. Developed in 1992, the beginning of the family’s work in Ein Sokhna. Built on 200,000 m² with 8% built-up area, it includes 91 villas and 192 chalets, and a 162-room hotel, Mövenpick Sokhna.

Ein Bay

A beachfront golf resort in Ein Sokhna, developed in 1998. Set on 1.7 million m² with a 1 million m² golf course and 50,000 m² of lagoons, and 7.5% built-up area. 297 villas and 1,226 chalets were built.

Residential buildings in Cairo

Luxury residential buildings in Dokki, Al-Azhar, Maadi and the Fifth Settlement.
